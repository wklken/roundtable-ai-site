ROUNDTABLE AI · MACOS

Support

Start with a question. Add context, choose your perspectives, then follow the discussion and decision report.

Connect your model

Open Settings → Models. Add a compatible provider, endpoint, API key and model. Review the AI data-sharing notice and allow the connection before testing or generating. Your provider bills model usage separately from the app purchase.

A request fails or stalls

Check your endpoint, selected model, API key, provider balance and network connection. Review AI permission in Settings → Models if you previously declined or revoked it. Pause a discussion before changing its context; continue when ready. A model provider outage or rate limit can interrupt generation.

Restore lifetime access

Open Settings → Lifetime unlock and choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. The app verifies the purchase with StoreKit. A refunded or revoked purchase no longer grants unlimited discussions.

Free discussions and history

Five completed discussions are free. A discussion counts once when its first report is saved; continuing it does not consume another free discussion. Existing history and Markdown export remain available without buying lifetime access.

Contact support

Email wklken@gmail.com for app support.

Include your macOS version, app version, what you expected and the error message. Remove API keys, passwords and private conversation content from screenshots or logs.
