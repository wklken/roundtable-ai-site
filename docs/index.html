A few things to know.

How is this different from a single AI reply?

Your question becomes a moderated discussion. Perspectives take turns, read earlier contributions and explore trade-offs before the moderator produces a decision report. Multiple perspectives don’t guarantee accuracy—verify important information.

Does the lifetime unlock include AI usage?

No. The one-time purchase unlocks unlimited discussions in the app. You provide your own compatible model connection, and your provider bills any usage separately.

Is this a subscription?

No. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase, not a recurring subscription. AI provider usage is billed separately.

Can I use it for important decisions?

Use it to organize your thinking and find questions worth asking. AI can be wrong or biased. Verify important claims and seek qualified professional advice where appropriate. You make the final decision.
