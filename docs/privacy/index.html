LITTLE ROUNDTABLE · MACOS

Privacy policy

Your discussion history stays on your Mac. AI requests go directly to the model connection you choose, after your permission.

Last updated: September 12, 2026. This policy describes the Little Roundtable macOS app, provided by Kunliang Wu.

Data stored on your Mac

The app stores conversations, generated reports, expert settings, model connections and preferences locally. It does not provide an app account or cloud synchronization service. API keys are encrypted locally, with the encryption key stored on the same Mac. This does not encrypt your entire conversation history; protect your Mac and backups appropriately.

Data sent for AI requests

When you permit a connection, the app sends the information needed for model discovery and generation directly to that endpoint. This can include your question, background you supply, conversation history, earlier model replies, expert instructions and model settings. The API key is sent as authentication. The provider also receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Later speakers can receive earlier discussion content to respond in context.

The consent screen identifies the connection and endpoint. You choose the provider, including a compatible local endpoint where supported. The developer does not operate a proxy for these requests and does not receive your conversations or keys through an app backend.

Your provider's processing

Providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. Retention, training use, account association and processing countries depend on the provider, endpoint and your account settings. The app does not promise zero retention or prevent a provider from keeping data already received. Review your provider's policy before sending personal or confidential information.

Your choices

You can decline AI permission, review it in Settings → Models, or revoke it to prevent future requests. Revocation does not undo completed transfers or recall an in-flight request. You can export reports and delete local conversations using the app. Separately remove exported files and backups you no longer need. Contact the provider for requests about data it retains.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and payments. The app reads verified StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock or restore features. The developer does not receive your payment-card information through the app. Apple's own privacy policy applies to its services.

Other network activity

Provider icons may be downloaded from GitHub and cached locally. Those requests disclose ordinary network information to the hosting service; they do not include your API keys or discussion text. Opening external links uses your browser. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Support and this website

If you contact support, the developer receives the message and the contact details you choose to provide, for handling your request. Do not send API keys or passwords. This website has no advertising, analytics scripts or conversation-upload form. Its hosting service processes ordinary network requests to serve the pages. Your language choice is stored in this browser so the website can remember it.
